AGMENTIC COMPASS

Most companies don't have an AI problem. They have a "where do we start" problem.

Compass is a working session where we map your company together and find the places where AI actually makes sense — before you spend money building anything.

Start a conversation See how it works

THE PATTERN

We've spent the past year in rooms with founders and executives across Zurich, Munich, Hamburg, Berlin and Stuttgart. The pattern is almost always the same: everyone agrees AI matters. Almost nobody can point to where it fits in their own company.

The technical teams can build. The budget exists. But nobody can answer the two questions that actually matter: build what — and why that, first?

Compass exists to close that gap.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Map

We sit down with you and map how your company actually works today. Processes, decisions, data, people. No slides from us, no pitch. Just questions.

02

Find the layers

Together we find the layers where AI can carry real weight — and just as importantly, where it can't yet. You leave with a clear picture, not a wish list.

03

Decide

You get a short, honest document: what's worth doing now, what to watch, and what to ignore. If you want to build something, we can help. If not, the map is yours.

WHAT YOU LEAVE WITH

A map of your AI opportunities

Made with you, not for you. Your team can read it, defend it, and act on it without us in the room.

A realistic view of where AI is going

What the next two to three years likely mean for your industry — without the hype and without the doom.

Clear priorities

Now. Later. Not worth it. Three lists, honestly sorted.

No dependency

The outcome is yours. There's no subscription hiding behind this, and no lock-in.

WHO IT'S FOR

Mittelstand companies and teams who know AI matters but don't want to guess their way into it. You don't need a technical team to start. You need two or three people who know how your company really works — and a few honest hours.

WHY US

We build AI systems ourselves at Agmentic Lab, and we host roundtables with founders and executives across Europe. We see what works in practice and what quietly fails. Compass is that experience, applied to your company.
